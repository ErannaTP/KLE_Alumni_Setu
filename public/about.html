About Us

Connecting Excellence, Building Futures

🏛️ Our Legacy: Excellence Since 1947

KLE Tech alumni are proud graduates of KLE Technological University (formerly B.V. Bhoomaraddi College of Engineering and Technology), an institution with a remarkable legacy spanning over seven decades. Established in 1947, KLE Tech has built a decades-long tradition of producing global leaders, innovators, and industry-ready professionals who shape the future across diverse fields.

Our institution stands as a beacon of Innovation and Entrepreneurship, fostering a culture where creativity meets practical application. Guided by our institutional motto, "Creating Value and Leveraging Knowledge," we empower every graduate to not only excel in their careers but to make meaningful contributions to society and industry.

🎯 Our Mission: Connect & Build Careers

The KLE Tech Alumni Network platform is dedicated to facilitating continuous Student-Alumni Career Connections that transcend graduation. Our central goal is career readiness through mentorship and opportunity sharing. We believe that the strongest foundation for professional success is built through the transfer of real-world experience, industry insights, and meaningful relationships between those who have walked the path and those just beginning their journey.

🌟 Our Community Philosophy

We believe that career development is a lifelong journey, not a destination. Success is rarely achieved in isolation—it flourishes when knowledge is shared, connections are nurtured, and opportunities are passed forward. Our network provides meaningful ways for successful alumni to contribute to the next generation, ensuring that today's students become tomorrow's mentors.

This platform embodies the spirit of paying it forward—every connection made, every piece of advice shared, and every opportunity extended strengthens the entire KLE Tech community. Together, we create a virtuous cycle of growth, success, and contribution.

This platform is developed under the esteemed guidance of Prof. Sunita Hiremath, whose vision and dedication continue to inspire excellence in our community.
